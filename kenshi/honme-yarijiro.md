> Published at <https://jikishinkageryu.org/kenshi/honme-yarijiro.html>.

Honme Nuijirō (本目縫次郎) — recorded in the primary appointment register as Nuinosuke (本目縫之助), elsewhere as Yarijirō (本目鑓次郎), and argued by the *Mata-shichi* blog to be properly Nuijirō (本目縫次郎) — was a [Jikishinkage-ryū](../ryuha/jikishinkage-ryu.md) swordsman attached to the Odani (男谷) house, with the notation *ni-jutsu* (二術; two arts/techniques) marking that he also held a spear post (he is thus one of the few men to appear on both the kenjutsu and the sōjutsu sides of the institute).

He served as *shihan-dai* (師範代; senior assistant instructor) at the Honjo Kamezawa-chō (本所亀沢町) dōjō of [Odani Seiichirō](odani-seiichiro.md) Nobutomo — the proposer of the Kōbusho, thirteenth-generation head of the line and heir to that dōjō from his own teacher Danno Gennoshin (団野源之進; sobriquet Shinpansai, 真帆斎). He is generally identified with the "Honme" of Nagakura Shinpachi's *Shinsen-gumi tenmatsu-ki*: in that episode, when Kondō Isami and his companions came to the Odani dōjō for a cross-school bout, Honme, standing in for the house, knocked away Kondō's *shinai* but, seeing Kondō take an unarmed stance, withdrew with a bow and an acknowledgement of his opponent's skill.

His tie to the Odani house was by marriage. He wed the eldest daughter of Odani Hikoshirō (男谷彦四郎), while Odani Seiichirō — who married the second daughter, Tsuru, and continued the Odani line — became his brother-in-law; since Seiichirō and Katsu Kaishū (勝海舟) were themselves cousins through the Odani line, Honme stood as a relation by marriage to Katsu as well. His year of birth is unrecorded, but he appears to have belonged to Seiichirō's and Katsu's generation: in the eleventh month of Ansei 3 his son Honme Tomigorō (本目富五郎) was already being appointed a Kōbusho kenjutsu *sewa-kokoroe* (世話心得; supervisory assistant), which implies Honme was a good deal senior to Kondō.

One period source names him independently of the appointment register. The *Fujiokaya nikki*, quoted in the institutional history of the Kōbusho on the schools and transmissions of the men who entered service as *kyōju-kata*, lists 「直心影流男谷精一郎門人書院番水野山城守組本目縫次郎」 — Honme Nuijirō of Mizuno Yamashiro-no-kami's company in the *shoin-ban*, a Jikishinkage-ryū pupil of Odani Seiichirō.[^fujiokaya-honme] This corroborates the school and the Odani pupillage from outside the *Mata-shichi* reconstruction, and it prints the name in the 縫次郎 form filed here rather than the register's 縫之助 — which does not settle the reading, but does show that the 縫次郎 form was current in a contemporary diary.

Beyond the marriage tie, the spear post and the *Shinsen-gumi tenmatsu-ki* anecdote, little about Honme is independently documented, and even his name is unsettled; the points above rest on the *Mata-shichi* reconstruction from the appointment record and Nagakura's memoir rather than on a dedicated biography.

A separate Honme should not be read into the Kōbusho records: the 本目長門守 who appears in the *Rikugun rekishi* as a unit commander is Honme Chikatami (本目親民), a *kojūnin-gashira*, and not the swordsman.

[^fujiokaya-honme]: 東京市史外編, 講武所 (NDL 1225131, image 46, printed p. 27), quoting the 藤岡屋日記.
