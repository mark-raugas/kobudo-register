After Ōnishi Hidetaka revived his lineage of [Jikishinkage-ryū](../ryuha/jikishinkage-ryu.md), different groups record the lineage that follows differently. It is known that the Hyakurenkai was subsequently founded in 1973. Kobudo organizations recognized the Hyakurenkai as the official line of Jikishinkage-ryū Odani-ha, while Kashima Jingū recognized Namiki Yasushi (1926–1999) as the next head.  
    
Namiki and Itō Masayuki (1930–2001) had both trained under [Kawashima](kawashima.md) — Namiki also trained in Heki-ryū  Sekka-ha Kyujutsu under Kawashima; Itō may have as well. Both groups stemming from the Odani faction of Jikishinkage-ryū (i.e., the Hyakurenkai and Namiki’s Hōjōkan) trained it seems mostly separately from 1974 onward. In Namiki’s case, his death in 1999 left Itō Masayuki in place only for two years as his successor before he too passed in 2001.  Namiki Kazuya (並木和也) continued training in Jikishinkage-ryū as the Kūunkai, while the dōtō passed to Yoshida Hijime as 20th generation.
