Matsushita Seiichirō (松下誠一郎) was a [Shingyōtō-ryū](../ryuha/shingyoto-ryu.md) swordsman who trained under Iba Gunbei Hidenari (伊庭軍兵衛秀業) at the Renbukan. Beyond his appointment as a Kōbusho kenjutsu *kyōju-kata* in Ansei 3 (1856) and his membership in the Iba school, little detail about him is recoverable from the accessible sources; he is one of the more thinly documented of the eleven.
