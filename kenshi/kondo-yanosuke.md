Kondō Yanosuke (近藤弥之助) was the [Chūya-ha Ittō-ryū](../ryuha/chuya-itto-ryu.md) representative among the eleven initial kenjutsu instructors at the Kōbusho; the appointment record places him under the supervision of Matsudaira Uneme. The sources are consistent on his school but offer little personal detail, and he is best treated as thinly attested.
