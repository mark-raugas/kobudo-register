> Published at <https://innerdharma.org/kenshi/monna-tadashi.html>.

Monna Tadashi is [Naitō Takaharu](naito-takaharu.md)'s counterpart: the second of the two Mito swordsmen whom [Shimoe Hidetarō](shimoe-hidetaro.md) brought into the Keishichō and who then became a pillar of Butokukai kendō.

He trained at the Mito Tōbukan (東武館) under Ozawa Torakichi, and while Shimoe taught there in 1885–1887 became his student, later reputed to have inherited Shimoe's [Hokushin Ittō-ryū](../ryuha/hokushin-ryu.md) technique most fully ([jawiki_shimoe](../references.md#jawiki_shimoe)). 

In 1887 (Meiji 20) Shimoe called him to Tokyo, secured his Keishichō post, and assigned him to the Tomioka-monzen station alongside himself, where Naitō joined them the following year ([jawiki_shimoe](../references.md#jawiki_shimoe)). He served as a gekken sewakari and rose within the force's kenjutsu establishment. 

In 1911 (Meiji 44) he was named — with Naitō Takaharu, Ozawa Ichirō, and Sasaki Masayoshi, all Tōbukan men — to the committee that drafted the Dai Nippon Teikoku Kendō Kata (大日本帝国剣道形), and is recorded among its chief drafting members (*shusa-iin* 主査委員; chief drafting member) ([jawiki_mito_tobukan](../references.md#jawiki_mito_tobukan)).[^busen-247]

[^busen-247]: On the Busen and its teachers, see the Kenshi247 essay "[Busen and koshi](https://kenshi247.net/blog/2018/09/10/busen-and-koshi/)."

He rose to the level of Dai Nippon Butokukai kendō hanshi; with Naitō he carried the Hokushin Ittō-ryū / Mito line into the leadership of late-Meiji-to-early-Shōwa kendō ([jawiki_hokushin_ittoryu](../references.md#jawiki_hokushin_ittoryu)).

Monna is thinly documented individually; he appears in the sources as a member of the Tōbukan kata-committee group rather than in his own right, so what is known of him comes from the Tōbukan, Shimoe, and police-history materials.
