## Summary

Komai Hangorō (駒井半五郎) was one of the Kōbusho's [Hōzōin-ryū](../ryuha/hozoin-ryu.md) spear instructors, serving from the
Ansei-period roster onward and rising to *shihan-yaku* at the end of Bunkyū 2 (1862). He is the only
Kōbusho spear teacher for whom the sources name both his school and a pupil of his own on the
premises, which makes him a transmission node rather than simply a name on a staff list.

## School and unit

The *Fujiokaya nikki*, quoted in the institutional history of the academy on the schools and
transmissions of the men who entered service as *kyōju-kata*, enters him as a Hōzōin-ryū man of Yokota
Chikugo-no-kami's company in the *koshōgumi*.[^fujiokaya] He belongs, in other words, to the
overwhelming Hōzōin majority of the spear department — eleven of its twelve instructors — rather than
to any of the minority lines.

A caution for anyone reading that list in transcription: 横田筑後守組 there is Komai's guard company,
not a separate instructor named Yokota Chikugo, and the tag "pupil of Nagao Kohei" (長尾小兵衛門人) that stands nearby belongs
to Katsu Kenzaburō, not to Komai.

## Career

The *shihan-yaku* register records his appointment on Bunkyū 2 (1862)/12/27, coming from the
*koshōgumi* company of Oda Iga-no-kami, to *shihan-yaku* with the standing of *ryōban jōseki*; and on
Bunkyū 3 (1863)/8/18, for diligent service, the grant of *nimaru rusui-kaku hoi*.[^rk147] He appears
in the spear section of the Kōbusho roster and again among the senior staff of Bunkyū 3.[^roster]

## His pupil

His one recorded pupil is [Ido Kinpei](ido-kinpei.md), described as a man of about thirty
"who handled the Hōzōin-ryū spear well," and who from beneath Komai in the *sewa-kokoroe* tier
proceeded to throw the entire teaching staff with a foot-entanglement until [Takahashi Deishū](deishu.md) put a
stop to it.[^tsg39] Komai's own conduct in that affair is not recorded.
