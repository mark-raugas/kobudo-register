## Summary

Hiraiwa Jirōdayū (平岩次郎太夫) was one of the four *shutsuyaku* appointed when the Kōbusho was
established in Ansei 2 (1855), and afterwards a *shihan-yaku* in the spear department. He is in the
register for a structural rather than a technical reason: he was there from the first day, in a
working post, and the sources that record the academy's founding record him by name. What they do not
record is any school, teacher or transmission — an absence worth stating plainly, since almost every
other spear teacher on the staff is entered with a line.

## The founding appointment

The announcement of Ansei 2 (1855)/2/5 names ten *sōsai* holding office concurrently, six men serving
jointly as *tōdori*, and beneath them four *shutsuyaku* who worked under the direction of the
superintendent and directors: Sugiura Shume of the *koshōgumi*, Kimura Kansuke, Hiraiwa Jirōdayū of
the *shinban* company of Matsudaira Shinano-no-kami, and Shōda Kinnosuke of the sword office.[^tsg10]
The same four appear in the *Rikugun rekishi* notice of the academy's establishment.[^rk79]

He was later among the senior staff who received the shogunal gifts distributed after the shogun's
visit: the *tōdori* and *shihan-yaku* took five scroll-rolls apiece, and Hiraiwa is listed among that
tier, entered by his *shinban* unit — alongside Odani, Ōkusa, Hasegawa, Kubota, Shimosone, Udono and
Egawa. The *kyōju-kata* below them received three bolts of cloth and the *sewa-kokoroe* two.[^tsg61]

He appears in the spear section of the roster as *shihan-yaku*,[^roster] and the *Yūgeki-tai* notices
of Keiō 2 (1866)/11/18 carry a Hiraiwa under the title Suruga-no-kami; whether that is this man is not
established.

## The Akasaka yakko

He was known in Edo as the *Akasaka yakko* (赤坂奴) — roughly "the Akasaka tough," *yakko* carrying
the flavor of a swaggering retainer-servant rather than a compliment. The by-name comes down through
a contemporary *rakushu* lampooning the academy and its people, where the epithet is glossed
directly: 「「赤坂奴」は鎗術平岩次郎太夫を指し」 — "the Akasaka yakko" means the spear instructor
Hiraiwa Jirōdayū. It stands in a paired set of such jibes, its companion 「端反の裏金」 being aimed at
Abe Seishū.[^tsg48] It is the only thing resembling a personal
characterization that the record preserves of him, and it suggests a figure conspicuous enough in the
city to be worth a satirist's shorthand.
