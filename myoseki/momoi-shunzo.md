The Shigakukan (士学館) headship, held under the succession-name *Momonoi Shunzō* (桃井春蔵;
furigana もものい), was one of the three great Edo dōjō (江戸三道場) alongside the Genbukan and the
Renpeikan. The name was carried by four masters of Kyōshin Meichi-ryū (鏡新明智流). A bare "Momonoi
Shunzō" almost always means the fourth holder, [Naomasa](../ryuha/kyoshin-meichi-ryu.md), under
whom the school recovered from decline and won its lasting reputation for *kurai* (位; poise) —
"poise, that is Momonoi." The individual holders are told apart by imina (諱): Naoyoshi (直由) →
Naokazu (直一) → Naoo (直雄) → Naomasa (直正).
